# Staffing plan and Saudization position

- Current headcount: 40, of whom 14 are Saudi nationals (35%).
- Our HR team's internal Nitaqat estimate puts the company in a green band today, with roughly 33% Saudization as the line for keeping that band at our size and activity. This is an internal estimate and has not been re-checked against the current MHRSD rules.
- The proposed plan (10 expatriate and 2 Saudi hires) takes headcount to 52 with 16 Saudi nationals (about 31%), below that internal line.
- Falling a band would restrict new work visas, which the second hiring wave depends on.
- An alternative mix of 8 expatriate and 4 Saudi hires gives 18 of 52 (about 35%). Recruiters say the 2 extra Saudi hires would add about 6 weeks to the first wave.
- Legal has not reviewed the plan.
