# People impact — weekend shift change

- Fully opt-in: 9 staff volunteered for 6 needed slots; no one is being assigned against their preference.
- Time-and-a-half pay for weekend hours already exists in the employee handbook as a standard policy — no new compensation structure needed.
- No headcount change, no restructuring, no role changes.
- Culture check: prior opt-in shift-flexibility programs (holiday coverage, 2023) had high satisfaction scores (4.6/5 in post-program survey) and no attrition impact.
- No contractual or legal exposure identified — shift scheduling is within standard at-will scheduling terms already in employee contracts.

**CHRO read:** low change-management load, opt-in only, no legal or retention risk identified.
