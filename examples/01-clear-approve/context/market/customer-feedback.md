# Customer feedback on support hours

- Q2 NPS survey: "lack of weekend support" is the #2 cited complaint (18% of respondents), behind only "response time on Mondays."
- 3 of our 4 named competitors already advertise weekend support coverage; we are the outlier.
- Support ticket data shows steady after-hours ticket submission on Saturdays (avg. 140 tickets/Saturday currently going unanswered until Monday).
- No indication this change creates customer confusion or expectation risk — it is purely additive to existing hours.

**CMO read:** closes a known competitive gap and addresses a top customer complaint; no brand or positioning downside identified.
