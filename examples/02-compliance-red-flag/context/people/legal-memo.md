# Memo from General Counsel — EU marketing list usage

**To:** Executive team
**From:** General Counsel
**Re:** 2019 EU contact list — marketing use restriction

This memo is a standing instruction, not a discussion point.

The 280,000-contact EU list imported in 2019 predates our current consent-tracking system and cannot be verified as GDPR-compliant for marketing use. Legal has already reviewed this specific list and determined:

1. This list **must not** be used for marketing sends until consent is re-confirmed on a per-contact basis or the list is re-permissioned through a compliant opt-in flow.
2. Using it as-is exposes the company to GDPR enforcement risk (fines of up to 4% of global annual revenue) and reputational risk if reported.
3. Any proposal to use this list requires **explicit legal sign-off before proceeding** — no exceptions for revenue timing pressure.

This is a blocker, not a risk to be weighed against upside. Route any urgent request to re-permission the list to Legal directly; do not proceed on the current timeline without sign-off.

**CHRO read:** this is exactly the kind of unreviewed legal/contractual exposure that must be flagged as a blocker to the council, independent of how strong the financial or market case looks.
